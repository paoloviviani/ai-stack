# Backlog: Pystino, Cerea and galopin

A single list of open work across the three projects. **This file is the
source of truth** until the items are filed as GitHub issues. When an item is
filed, its issue number goes next to it, and the item stays here as an index.

> **Every open item is a proposal, not an agreed plan.** Items were gathered
> from sessions, ADRs and reviews; the owner has not agreed with all of them
> and will go through them one by one. Until then nothing here is scheduled,
> filed or worked on because it is listed. After the review, each item gets
> one of: **agreed**, **rejected** (moved to "Rejected", with the reason) or
> **changed** (rewritten as decided). Items explicitly decided before
> (marked "(owner)" in their context) are the exception.

- **IDs:** **P** for Pystino (gateway and console), **C** for Cerea (the chat
  and the deploy kit), **G** for galopin (the machine agent, in Cerea's
  `agent/`). IDs are stable: a finished item moves to "Done", it is never
  renumbered.
- **Types:**
  - `feature`;
  - `fix`;
  - `investigation`;
  - `chore`;
  - `parked`: designed or decided, deliberately not started;
  - `upstream`: someone else's bug, which we work around or report.
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-27, at Cerea 0.31.1 and Pystino 0.18.1 released; Cerea 0.32.0 in progress (C30 pptx editing merged, C31 connectors). Stale plans and briefs archived to `~/workspace/archive/2026-10/` (audit: `2026-10-27-stale-plans-audit.md`).
